SAV-SLAM

Semantic audio-visual SLAM

Semantic audio-visual SLAM that connects sound sources to physical objects. Built around an iPhone 16 Pro and a ~$60 four-microphone array.

Multimodal perceptionVGGT-SLAMCLAPPyTorch
GitHub ↗
How it works +

VGGT-SLAM and YOLO build an object-centric map; CLAP and text-prompted separation discover sound streams. A shared time-frequency mask weights the raw microphone channels while preserving relative phase. SRP-PHAT bearings are mapped into image columns for attribution and source discovery. The system reports ambiguous or unattributed streams when evidence is insufficient; objects sharing a bearing remain a known limitation.

LoopLens

Visual loop closure & pose-graph optimization

Detects revisited places, estimates relative poses, and corrects trajectory drift. Combines DINOv2 retrieval, DISK + LightGlue matching, stereo-depth PnP, and a custom SE(3) pose-graph optimizer.

DINOv2LightGluePnP + RANSACSE(3)
GitHub ↗
Implementation & evaluation +

Implemented a sparse Gauss–Newton optimizer on SE(3), including Lie-group updates, analytical Jacobians, and separate translation and rotation weights. Loop constraints come from stereo depth and geometrically verified image matches.

Evaluated offline on all 4,541 frames of KITTI 00, using an ORB-SLAM3 stereo trajectory with its loop-closing thread disabled. ATE RMSE is measured after rigid alignment without scale correction. These results cover one sequence; online operation has not been evaluated.

A separate Gardens Point study compares CNN and transformer retrieval, causal sequence matching, and geometric verification. Acceptance thresholds are fitted on validation data and frozen before test, keeping ranking improvements separate from acceptance reliability.
